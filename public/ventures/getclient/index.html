GetClient

Hire local. Hire checked.

GetClient is South Africa’s verified business directory, built by Digitalx. People find checked businesses and request quotes; good businesses get a way to prove they are trustworthy.

The challenge

People searching for a service provider struggle to tell real, registered businesses from the rest. Good businesses, in turn, struggle to prove they are trustworthy. GetClient is Digitalx's answer to both.

Our approach

Verification first, shown in the open. Every profile lists which checks it passed and when; checks expire after 12 months and are re-run, so a badge never goes stale.

What we built

  • Search by business, category and city, plus location and category pages
  • Up to five checks per profile: identity, CIPC registration, insurance, trading history and client ratings
  • Free listings and paid Blue and Gold verification tiers, with public pricing
  • "Listed on GetClient" badges that businesses add to their own sites with one line of code
  • Quote requests, and a light and dark theme
  • An installable web app

The outcome

  • A live directory where people find checked businesses and request quotes
  • Verified businesses show their badge on their own websites, as Digitalx and several of our clients do
