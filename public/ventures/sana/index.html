SANA

Safe and Noble Actions. Every trip. Every driver. Every time.

SANA (Safe and Noble Actions) is a road-safety awareness initiative for South African roads, started by Keith through Digitalx as a community initiative.

Visit sana.digitalxagency.ioInitiative
Type
Community initiative
Reach
South Africa
By
Digitalx

The challenge

Road-safety advice is often long, technical and easy to ignore. As our own community initiative, SANA needed a simple, memorable home for practical guidance that anyone can act on and share.

Our approach

Short topics, one-line tips and a clear invitation to get involved, built around the tagline "Every trip. Every driver. Every time."

What we built

  • Eight road-safety topic pages, from speeding and drunk driving to fatigue and pedestrian awareness
  • Shareable safety tips and a campaigns section
  • Free resources to download
  • Get-involved paths for individuals, businesses, schools and communities

The outcome

  • A free public resource that schools, businesses and drivers can use and share
  • A home for SANA campaigns as they launch

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Mobile-first design
  • SEO set-up
