Three ways to take a brand to the street

Bike advertising

From R2,000 per bike per month. Courier and food-delivery bikes become rolling billboards in your target suburbs.

Vehicle branding

Full or partial premium vinyl wraps for sedans, SUVs, bakkies and vans.

Fleet branding

Consistent branding across 5 vehicles or 500, with volume pricing and nationwide deployment.

The Go Ads difference

  • Pricing published up front: from R2,000 per bike per month
  • Book from a single bike, where others ask for 10 to 25
  • 12-month or month-to-month contracts
  • Bikes, vehicle wraps and full fleets in one campaign
  • An interactive campaign planner to see reach and budget before calling anyone
  • Artwork guidance, deployment and a performance report every month

Live on the road in four steps

  1. Choose a package, or build your own in the planner
  2. Submit your artwork, with guidance on sizing and production
  3. The team wraps the bikes or vehicles and deploys them in your zones
  4. Track results monthly, then scale up, switch zones or renew
