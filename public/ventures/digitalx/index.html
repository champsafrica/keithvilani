Four pillars

Every Digitalx engagement sits on one or more of four pillars, each sold with published prices and looked after month by month.

Web Development

Fast, mobile-first websites that look professional, show up on Google and bring in enquiries.

Domains & Hosting

Your domain, hosting, SSL and business email in one place, looked after every month.

Custom Software

Web apps, client portals and dashboards built around the way your business works.

Company Registration

Your Pty Ltd registered with CIPC, with help opening a business bank account, and kept compliant.

How Digitalx works

  • Plain language: every service explained without jargon
  • Prices published up front, not hidden behind a sales call
  • No invented results: case studies describe only what is visible or was delivered

  • Sites are hosted and maintained after launch, so information stays current
