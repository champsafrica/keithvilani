Advera

Tell us about your business. Let AI build your campaign. Approve it. Launch it.

Advera is an AI-powered advertising platform for small and medium businesses and agencies, starting in South Africa. Businesses describe what they sell, the AI drafts a strategy and platform-specific ads, a person approves the exact version, and Advera publishes it and brings the results back into one dashboard.

How it works

  1. Describe your business and what you sell
  2. The AI drafts a strategy and ads for each platform
  3. A person approves the exact version: budget, schedule, accounts and copy
  4. Advera publishes through each platform’s official API
  5. Results come back into one dashboard

Safety built into the database, not just the screen

  • Nothing is published without approval of the exact current version
  • Any change creates a new version and withdraws earlier approvals
  • Every campaign has an end date, so every approval has a bounded maximum spend
  • Budgets are never raised automatically
  • Advertising account tokens are encrypted and never reach the browser
