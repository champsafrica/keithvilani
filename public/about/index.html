About

The builder behind the sky.

Keith Vilani is the founder and CEO of Digitalx, the Cape Town agency that builds the systems businesses need to launch, operate and grow. He is also the founder of Go Ads and the Keith Vilani Foundation, and the person behind SANA, GetClient and Advera.

Why I build

Every business deserves to look, sell and grow like the biggest names. So I build the systems that make it possible, then I turn those same tools toward the people and places that need them most. Profit and purpose, built by the same hands.

How I work

The same principles run through every brand on this site.

  1. Publish the price

    Go Ads, Digitalx and GetClient all show their prices up front. Trust starts before the first call.

  2. Prove it in the open

    GetClient shows every check a business passed. Digitalx case studies describe only what was delivered.

  3. Say it plainly

    Road safety, healthcare or visas: every page is written so anyone can act on it.

  4. Meet people where they are

    Mobile-first, and booking as easy as a WhatsApp message.

  5. Build for purpose too

    The same hands that build businesses build the Foundation and SANA.
