Mbeki Healthcare Clinic System

Custom clinic software, built and managed by Digitalx

A secure clinic administration system with one record per patient, from the front desk to the consulting room.

Private system for staffHealthcare
Sector
Healthcare
Where
Kuils River, Cape Town
What
Clinic administration system

The challenge

The practice needed one secure place to run the clinic, from registering patients at the front desk to clinical notes and consent in the consulting room, without expensive off-the-shelf software.

Our approach

Give every role its own station. Everything the front desk records is available to the nurse, but nothing clinical travels the other way, and every access to a patient record is logged.

What we built

  • One record per patient, from arrival to departure
  • Separate stations for the front desk, nurses and the practice administrator
  • Allergies, notes, diagnoses and medication hidden from the front desk
  • A log of every access to patient information
  • Document and PDF generation, and an installable app for staff

The outcome

  • Front desk, consulting room and consent handled in one system
  • Clinical information protected by role-based access
  • A full record of who accessed patient information

Technology

  • Bespoke web platform
  • Supabase with Row Level Security
  • Role-based access
  • Installable web app
  • PDF generation
