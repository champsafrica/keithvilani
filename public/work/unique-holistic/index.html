Unique Holistic Spa

Massage and wellness spa in Belgravia, Bellville

A calm spa website with a full price list, mobile massage across Cape Town and online booking.

Visit the live siteWellness
Sector
Wellness
Where
Bellville, Cape Town
What
Spa website with online booking

The challenge

Unique Holistic offers massage, lashes and nails, waxing, body scrubs, sauna and more from its Bellville spa, plus mobile massage across Cape Town. Clients wanted to see treatments and prices and book quickly, often from their phones.

Our approach

Wellness is chosen on feel, then on price. A calm design sets the tone, the full price list is easy to find, and a sticky call, WhatsApp and book bar keeps booking within reach on every screen.

What we built

  • Treatment, pricing, mobile massage and team pages
  • Online booking through Fresha, plus call and WhatsApp options
  • Area pages for the Northern Suburbs
  • FAQs, opening hours and directions
  • Hosting and ongoing management by Digitalx

The outcome

  • Clients can compare treatments and prices and book online in a few taps
  • Mobile massage, with its travel fee, is explained before anyone asks
  • Local area pages help the spa appear in nearby searches

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Mobile-first design
  • Fresha booking
  • Local SEO
  • WhatsApp
