TechStep Academy

Practical computer and technology training, Blackheath, Cape Town

A course website that helps learners find their level, see every price and register or enrol on WhatsApp.

Visit the live siteEducation
Sector
Education
Where
Blackheath, Cape Town
What
Training academy website

The challenge

TechStep Academy teaches practical computer and technology skills in five levels, from computer foundations to basic coding, for individuals and for company teams. Prospective learners needed to understand which level suits them, what it costs and how to start.

Our approach

Make the learning path obvious. Each level has a clear outcome and a published price, a short "find your level" tool removes the guesswork, and enrolment works the way people already communicate: online or on WhatsApp.

What we built

  • Programme, course and pricing pages for all five levels and the full pathway
  • A "find your level" tool and online registration
  • Instalment options explained up front: a deposit plus monthly payments
  • A refer-and-earn page and a downloadable company profile
  • Location pages for learners across all nine provinces
  • Hosting and ongoing management by Digitalx

The outcome

  • Learners can choose a level, see the price and register without phoning first
  • Companies can find on-site team training in the same place
  • Every course and location has its own page for search

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Mobile-first design
  • Technical SEO
  • WhatsApp enrolment
  • Online registration
