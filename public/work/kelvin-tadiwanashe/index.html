Kelvin Tadiwanashe

Author of "This Is Our Country: Between Memory and Tomorrow", now launched

The challenge

Kelvin Tadiwanashe, a Zimbabwean author, needed a home for his debut book, "This Is Our Country: Between Memory and Tomorrow", ahead of its launch: somewhere readers, press and the publisher could point to.

Our approach

Put the book first, then let the author speak. After the launch we rebuilt the site in an editorial style: a cinematic cover hero, the book's three themes, press coverage and quotes in Kelvin's own words, with "Get the book" always one tap away.

What we built

  • An editorial homepage: the launched book, "In the news", "In his own words" and a photo journey
  • The book's three threads (History & Memory, Leadership and Zimbabwean Identity) told as short sections
  • "Get the book" calls to action linking to the publisher
  • About, book, blog and contact pages, plus a photo gallery with a lightbox

The outcome

  • The site now announces the launched book and sends readers straight to buy it
  • Press coverage, quotes and launch photos sit together, ready for media and event organisers

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Editorial design
  • Mobile-first design
