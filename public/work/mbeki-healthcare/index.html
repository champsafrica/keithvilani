Mbeki Healthcare

A nurse-practitioner practice in Kuils River and King William's Town

A healthcare website that explains every service in plain language and makes booking an appointment simple.

Sector

Where

What
Healthcare website

The challenge

Mbeki Healthcare is a nurse-practitioner practice led by Sr Phindie Mbeki, with primary healthcare in Kuils River and a wellness and aesthetics branch in King William's Town. Patients needed one trustworthy place to see what the practice offers, which branch to visit and how to book.

Our approach

Lead with trust and clarity. Every service gets its own plain-language page, both branches are clearly separated, and booking is never more than one tap away, by form or WhatsApp.

What we built

  • A mobile-first website with 18 service pages, from chronic care and family planning to wound care and IV drips
  • Separate branch and area pages for Kuils River, King William's Town and surrounding suburbs
  • An appointment booking form and WhatsApp booking
  • A Google review helper that copies the patient's words and opens the review page
  • A page for the practice's Phindi Naturals product range
  • Hosting, SSL and business email, managed by Digitalx

The outcome

  • Patients can find the right service and branch and book online or on WhatsApp
  • Payment options, emergency contact and opening details are visible on every device
  • The site is maintained by Digitalx, so information stays current

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Mobile-first design
  • Local SEO set-up
  • Google Business Profile
  • WhatsApp booking
