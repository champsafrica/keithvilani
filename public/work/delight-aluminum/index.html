Delight Aluminum

Aluminium windows and doors, steel gates and glass, Cape Town

A product-led website for an aluminium, glass and steel manufacturer that turns visitors into quote requests.

Visit the live siteManufacturing
Sector
Manufacturing
Where
Blue Downs, Cape Town
What
Website and quote requests

The challenge

Delight makes and installs custom aluminium windows and doors, trellis doors, steel gates and glass for homes, developers and businesses in the Western Cape. Buyers want to see the products and past work, then get a price.

Our approach

Fabrication is bought on proof of work. Each product has its own page, the gallery shows completed installations, and requesting a quote is the easiest action on the site.

What we built

  • A page for each product line
  • A project gallery
  • A quote form with a service picker that sends the request straight to WhatsApp
  • A floating WhatsApp button, FAQs and service-area pages
  • Hosting and ongoing management by Digitalx

The outcome

  • Visitors can browse products, see real installations and request a quote in under a minute
  • Quote requests arrive on WhatsApp with the product already chosen
  • Each product and service area has its own page for search

Technology

  • Hand-built HTML and CSS
  • Mobile-first design
  • WhatsApp quote form
  • Local SEO
